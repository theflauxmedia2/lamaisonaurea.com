You are the **world’s leading luxury interior brand designer and digital art director**, known for crafting ultra-premium, timeless websites for elite interior studios like Axel Vervoordt, Vincent Van Duysen, and Studio KO.

I want you to **elevate the entire La Maison Aurea website** into what would be considered **the best interior design website in the world** — emotionally rich, editorial, and ultra-luxury.

### Overall Vision

* The website should feel **calm, slow, cinematic, and editorial**
* Luxury through **restraint**, not decoration
* Every section should feel like a **coffee-table design book**
* Prioritize **negative space, typography, and imagery**
* Nothing should feel “template” or “startup-like”

---

### 1. Hero Section (Critical Upgrade)

* Replace static hero with a **slow-moving cinematic slider or subtle parallax**
* Add **film-grain texture** and soft vignette
* Text animation should fade in gently (opacity + translateY)
* Reduce copy to fewer, stronger words
  Example:

  * “Timeless Luxury”
  * “Designed for the Way You Live”
* CTA button should be **ghost-style**, thin border, elegant hover micro-interaction

---

### 2. Typography (Major Redesign)

* Use **high-end serif for headings** (Canela / Playfair Display / Editorial New)
* Use **clean neutral sans-serif** for body (Inter / Neue Haas Grotesk style)
* Increase line height and letter spacing
* Headlines should feel **editorial, not marketing**
* Avoid bold fonts — luxury = light to regular weights

---

### 3. Color Palette & Mood

* Reduce contrast: no harsh whites or blacks
* Use warm neutrals:

  * Ivory / Stone / Sand / Soft Taupe
* Accent color: muted gold or warm bronze (very minimal)
* Backgrounds should feel like **plaster walls**, not flat white

---

### 4. Layout & Spacing

* Increase vertical spacing between sections (luxury breathes)
* Align content to a **strict grid system**
* Reduce number of visible elements per section
* Each section should have a clear emotional purpose

---

### 5. Philosophy / About Section

* Turn into a **storytelling editorial block**
* Use split layout:

  * One large immersive image
  * One poetic paragraph (not corporate text)
* Add subtle divider lines or quotes
* Make it feel like a page from an architecture monograph

---

### 6. Projects / Selected Works

* Show **fewer projects**, but larger
* Full-bleed images with minimal captions
* Hover reveals project name softly
* Remove excessive buttons — one strong “View All Works”
* Transitions must be smooth and slow

---

### 7. Micro-Interactions (Luxury Detail)

* Smooth hover states on text and buttons
* Cursor interaction (optional): subtle scale or underline reveal
* Page transitions should fade, not snap
* Avoid flashy animations — subtle = premium

---

### 8. Footer Redesign

* Treat footer as a **final editorial moment**
* Large serif brand name
* Minimal links
* Soft divider line
* Calm background tone
* Social links as text, not icons

---

### 9. Performance & Polish

* Optimize images for sharpness + softness balance
* Ensure consistent padding on all screen sizes
* Make mobile feel as luxurious as desktop
* No abrupt transitions, no visual noise

---

### Final Goal

When someone opens this site, they should feel:

* Calm
* Trust
* Exclusivity
* Emotional connection
* “This studio understands luxury at a deeper level”

Implement these changes directly in the codebase while preserving existing content structure unless refinement is necessary.
