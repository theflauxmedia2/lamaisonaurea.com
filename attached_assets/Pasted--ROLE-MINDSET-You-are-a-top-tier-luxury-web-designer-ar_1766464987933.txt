🧠 ROLE & MINDSET

You are a top-tier luxury web designer, art director, and UX strategist specializing in high-end interior design brands.
Your work feels editorial, architectural, emotional, and timeless — never trendy or loud.

Your goal is to design a complete multi-page website that feels:
	•	Visually rich
	•	Calm & effortless
	•	Emotion-driven
	•	Premium & luxurious

⸻

🏛️ BRAND OVERVIEW

Design a full website for La Maison Aurea, a luxury interior design studio.

Brand Philosophy
	•	Timeless luxury
	•	Soulful interiors
	•	Harmony & balance
	•	Emotional resonance
	•	Quiet elegance

Brand Voice
	•	Soft
	•	Poetic
	•	Confident
	•	Minimal
	•	Refined

⸻

🎨 COLOR PALETTE

Use only neutral luxury tones:
	•	Primary: Soft White / Ivory
	•	Secondary: Charcoal Black
	•	Accent: Warm Brown / Light Brown / Earthy Beige
	•	Optional: Muted gold or bronze (very subtle)

⚠️ Avoid bright colors, gradients, neon, or flashy effects.

⸻

✒️ TYPOGRAPHY
	•	Headings: Elegant serif (editorial, high-fashion feel)
	•	Body text: Clean modern sans-serif
	•	Generous line spacing
	•	Light to regular font weights
	•	Typography should feel breathable and expensive

⸻

🖼️ VISUAL DIRECTION (CRITICAL)

This website is IMAGE-LED, not text-heavy.
	•	Full-width luxury interior photography
	•	Editorial layouts
	•	Large margins & white space
	•	Soft transitions
	•	Images must dominate the experience
	•	Text supports visuals, never competes

Think luxury interior magazine meets architecture studio.

⸻
